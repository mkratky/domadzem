Domácí Džem is a trio of musicians bound together by a shared love for great music. Our biggest passions are British rock, pop, and soul, which is why our repertoire mainly features well-known international hits. However, we don't just do carbon copies; we give each song our own original twist.

Though there are only three of us, our sound will surprise you with its fullness and energy. We love blending the traditional with the modern.

The basslines are pleasantly smooth and melodic.

The punchy piano and rhythmic acoustic guitar give the songs a real groove and drive.

A digital wind instrument (aerophone) allows us to conjure up sounds you wouldn’t normally expect from a classic three-piece band.

And everything is 100% live! We pride ourselves on performing without any pre-recorded backing tracks or computer substitutes. Every single note you hear is created right there on stage in the moment—purely for the joy of connecting directly with you.
